FAQ

I opened the app but no window appears.

ColorMix Bar is a menu bar app, so it has no Dock icon or main window. Click the palette icon in the menu bar at the top right of your screen. If your menu bar is crowded, the icon may be hidden behind other icons.

How do I pick a color from the screen?

Click the blue eyedropper button, then click anywhere on screen (press Esc to cancel). The color is saved to the current palette and the menu bar icon briefly shows it. No Screen Recording permission is needed.

Can I make my own color?

Click + next to the eyedropper to open the color wheel. Choose a color, adjust brightness, and click “Add to Palette”.

How do I copy a color?

Click a color chip. Choose the format (HEX, RGB, HSL) in Settings › General › Copy Format. You can also right-click › Copy As, or drag a chip into a text field in another app.

How do I mix colors?

Drag color chips into the Mixing Dish. Drop the same color again to add more of it, and click a small dot in the dish to remove one. Click the result to copy it, or “Add to Palette” to keep it. Mixing works like paint: blue and yellow make green, not gray.

Can I have more than one palette?

Use + in the tab row to create a palette and click a tab to switch. Right-click a tab to rename, duplicate, or delete it, and drag a tab onto another to reorder.

How do I move a color to another palette?

Drag the chip onto another palette’s tab. Or right-click the chip › Copy Color, then right-click a tab or an empty area › Paste Color.

Can it stay open while I use other apps?

Click 📌 next to the title, or drag the title bar away from the menu bar, to turn it into a small floating window. Click 📌 again or close the window to go back.

How do I back up palettes or move them to another Mac?

Export palettes as .json files in Settings › Palettes and import them on the other Mac. With the same Apple Account, you can also turn on iCloud Sync.

iCloud Sync isn’t working.

Make sure both Macs are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and that Settings › General › iCloud Sync is on. Changes can take a few minutes to appear. Turning sync on merges iCloud and local palettes.

How do I change the language or appearance?

Choose Appearance (Follow System/Light/Dark) and Language (Follow System/한국어/English/日本語) in Settings › General.

Can it start when I log in?

Turn on Settings › General › Launch at Login.

How do I reset the app?

In Settings › General › Reset, choose “Reset Everything” (settings and palettes) or “Reset Palettes” (keep settings). Deleted palettes can’t be restored.

How do I uninstall it?

If Launch at Login is on, turn it off first. Click “Quit” at the bottom of the popover, then move ColorMix Bar from Applications to the Trash.

I’d like a refund.

Purchases are handled by Apple. Please request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. The developer can’t issue refunds directly.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your Mac and, if you turn on sync, in your own iCloud. The developer can’t see it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
