Privacy Policy

Effective: October 23, 2026

The developer of ColorMix Bar (“we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what the ColorMix Bar macOS app (the “App”) stores, where, and how it is used.

Summary — ColorMix Bar has no accounts and no developer-operated servers. Your palettes and settings are stored only on your Mac and, if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own iCloud. We can’t see them. Payments are handled by Apple and we don’t receive payment information. The App has no ads, analytics, or tracking.

1. Information Stored

The App doesn’t collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email, phone number, or location. The following is stored on your Mac so the App can work.

PalettesPalette names and order, and the colors in them (sRGB values)
SettingsAppearance, language, copy format, pinned state, whether iCloud Sync is on, window position
Mixing DishColors being mixed are kept in memory only while the App is running and are not saved

2. How It’s Used

  • To save and load palettes, copy colors, and mix colors
  • To restore your palettes and settings when you reopen the App
  • To sync palettes between your Macs with the same Apple Account, if you turn on iCloud Sync

3. Where It’s Stored and for How Long

  • Your Mac: Palettes and settings are stored in the App’s sandboxed container, which other apps can’t access.
  • iCloud: If you turn on Settings › General › iCloud Sync, palettes (names, order, colors) are stored in Apple’s iCloud key-value storage in your own iCloud account. Settings are not synced. We can’t access this data.
  • Data is kept until you delete it or delete the App.

4. Deleting Your Data

You can view, edit, and delete everything yourself.

  • One color: right-click a chip › Delete
  • All colors in a palette: right-click an empty area of the chip area › Remove All Colors
  • A palette: right-click its tab › Delete
  • Everything at once: Settings › General › Reset (Reset Everything / Reset Palettes)
  • All data on your Mac: delete the App

Palettes deleted while iCloud Sync is on are also removed from iCloud and your other Macs. “Reset Everything” turns sync off first, so palettes in iCloud are kept. To remove them from iCloud too, use “Reset Palettes” while sync is on. Deleted data can’t be restored.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

We don’t sell or share your information and don’t send it to our servers. iCloud Sync is provided by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We don’t receive your name, Apple Account, or card details; App Store Connect only shows us anonymous totals such as sales by country.

6. Device Features and Permissions

  • Eyedropper: Uses the built-in macOS color sampler. The App receives only the color of the single point you click. It doesn’t request Screen Recording permission and doesn’t save or send screen images.
  • Clipboard: The App only writes color codes to the clipboard when you copy. It doesn’t read clipboard contents.
  • Files: When you export or import palettes, the App reads and writes only the files you choose.
  • Launch at Login: The App is added to macOS login items only if you turn this on.
  • The App doesn’t request location, photos, contacts, camera, microphone, or notification permissions, and doesn’t communicate with developer servers.

7. Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

The App contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs and doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. If you choose to share diagnostics with Apple, Apple may provide us with anonymous statistics.

8. Children

We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
